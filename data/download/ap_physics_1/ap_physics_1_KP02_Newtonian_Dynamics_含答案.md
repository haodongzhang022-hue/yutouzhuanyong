## 📖 参考答案与解析

**1. A 2 kg object is pushed with a constant net force of 10 N. What is the magnitude of the object's acceleration?**

- 答案：`B`
- 解析：Using Newton's 2nd law F=ma, a=F/m=10/2=5 m/s².

**2. A 5 kg box is pulled by a horizontal force of 25 N. The coefficient of kinetic friction is 0.2. What is the acceleration? (g = 10 m/s²)**

- 答案：`A`
- 解析：Friction f=μmg=0.2×5×10=10N. Net force F=25-10=15N. a=F/m=15/5=3 m/s².

**3. A block of mass m is on a frictionless incline at angle 30°. What is its acceleration down the incline? (g = 10 m/s²)**

- 答案：`B`
- 解析：a = g·sinθ = 10×sin30° = 10×0.5 = 5 m/s².
