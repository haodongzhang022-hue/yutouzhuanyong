## 📖 参考答案与解析

**1. A hydraulic lift has piston areas A₁ = 0.01 m² and A₂ = 1 m². A force of 100 N is applied to the small piston. What force is exerted by the large piston?**

- 答案：`D`
- 来源题号：pb-d1-004
- 解析：Pascal's principle: F₂ = F₁(A₂/A₁) = 100 × (1/0.01) = 10000 N.
