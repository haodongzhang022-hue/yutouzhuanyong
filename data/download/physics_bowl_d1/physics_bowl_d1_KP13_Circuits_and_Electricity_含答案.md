## 📖 参考答案与解析

**1. A circuit has a 12 V battery connected to a 4 Ω resistor. What is the current?**

- 答案：`B`
- 来源题号：pb-d1-005
- 解析：Ohm's law: I = V/R = 12/4 = 3 A.
