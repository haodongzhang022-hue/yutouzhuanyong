## 📖 参考答案与解析

**1. Two positive charges of 2 μC and 3 μC are separated by a distance of 0.1 m. What is the magnitude of the force between them? (k = 9×10⁹ N·m²/C²)**

- 答案：`A`
- 解析：F = kq₁q₂/r² = 9×10⁹×2×10⁻⁶×3×10⁻⁶/0.01 = 9×10⁹×6×10⁻¹²/0.01 = 54×10⁻³/0.01 = 5.4 N.
