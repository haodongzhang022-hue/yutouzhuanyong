## 📖 参考答案与解析

**1. A block slides down a frictionless incline of height h from rest. At the bottom, it compresses a spring of spring constant k. What is the maximum compression x of the spring?**

- 答案：`B`
- 解析：Energy conservation: mgh = ½kx², so x = √(2mgh/k).

**2. A 2 kg object is moving at 6 m/s. How much work is needed to stop it?**

- 答案：`D`
- 解析：Work = ΔKE = ½mv² = ½×2×36 = 36 J.

**3. A force of 50 N is applied to push a box 3 m along the floor at an angle of 60° above the horizontal. How much work is done?**

- 答案：`B`
- 解析：W = F·d·cosθ = 50×3×cos60° = 150×0.5 = 75 J.

**4. A machine does 5000 J of work in 20 seconds. What is its power output?**

- 答案：`B`
- 解析：P = W/t = 5000/20 = 250 W.
