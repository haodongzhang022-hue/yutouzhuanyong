## 📖 参考答案与解析

**1. Three identical resistors of resistance R are connected in parallel. What is the equivalent resistance?**

- 答案：`B`
- 解析：For n equal resistors in parallel, Req = R/n = R/3.

**2. A resistor has a voltage of 12 V across it and a current of 0.5 A flows through it. What is the resistance?**

- 答案：`C`
- 解析：R = V/I = 12/0.5 = 24 Ω.
