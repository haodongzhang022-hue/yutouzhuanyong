## 📖 参考答案与解析

**1. Two carts of equal mass on a frictionless track collide. Cart A moving at 4 m/s hits stationary cart B in a perfectly inelastic collision. What is their combined speed after collision?**

- 答案：`B`
- 解析：Conservation of momentum: m·4 = 2m·v, so v=2 m/s.

**2. A 0.5 kg ball is dropped from rest from a height of 2 m onto a hard floor. It rebounds to a height of 1.5 m. Calculate: (a) the impulse delivered to the ball by the floor, and (b) the average force if contact time is 0.01 s. (g = 10 m/s²)**

- 答案：`(a) J = Δp ≈ 5.4 N·s upward; (b) F_avg = J/Δt = 540 N`
- 解析：v_down = √(2gh)=√40≈6.32 m/s, v_up = √(2gh')=√30≈5.48 m/s. J = m(v_up+v_down)=0.5×(6.32+5.48)=5.9 N·s.

**3. A 0.2 kg hockey puck moving at 10 m/s hits a stationary 0.3 kg puck in an elastic collision. What is the speed of the 0.2 kg puck after the collision?**

- 答案：`B`
- 解析：For elastic collision with m2 at rest: v1' = (m1-m2)/(m1+m2)×v1 = (0.2-0.3)/(0.5)×10 = -2 m/s (reverse direction). Speed = 2 m/s.
