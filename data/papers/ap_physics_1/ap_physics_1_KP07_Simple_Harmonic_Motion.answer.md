**1. A mass-spring system oscillates with period T. If the mass is doubled, what happens to the period?**

- A. It stays the same
- B. It increases by a factor of √2
- C. It doubles
- D. It halves

---

**2. A pendulum has a length of 1 m. What is its period? (g = 10 m/s², π²≈10)**

- A. 1 s
- B. 2 s
- C. 3 s
- D. 4 s

---


## 📖 参考答案与解析

**1. A mass-spring system oscillates with period T. If the mass is doubled, what happens to the period?**

- 答案：`B`
- 解析：T = 2π√(m/k). Doubling m makes T increase by √2.

**2. A pendulum has a length of 1 m. What is its period? (g = 10 m/s², π²≈10)**

- 答案：`B`
- 解析：T = 2π√(L/g) = 2π√(1/10) ≈ 2π/π ≈ 2 s.
