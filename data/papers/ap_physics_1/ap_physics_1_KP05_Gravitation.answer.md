## 一、单选题（共 2 题）

**1. A satellite orbits Earth at a constant speed in a circular orbit. What is the direction of its acceleration?**

- A. In the direction of motion
- B. Opposite to its motion
- C. Toward the center of Earth
- D. There is no acceleration

---

**2. A planet has mass M and radius R. The acceleration due to gravity at its surface is g. If the planet's radius doubles while keeping mass the same, what is the new surface gravity?**

- A. g/4
- B. g/2
- C. 2g
- D. 4g

---


## 📖 参考答案与解析

**1. A satellite orbits Earth at a constant speed in a circular orbit. What is the direction of its acceleration?**

- 答案：`C`
- 解析：In uniform circular motion, acceleration (centripetal) points toward the center.

**2. A planet has mass M and radius R. The acceleration due to gravity at its surface is g. If the planet's radius doubles while keeping mass the same, what is the new surface gravity?**

- 答案：`A`
- 解析：g = GM/R². If R doubles, g' = GM/(2R)² = GM/(4R²) = g/4.
