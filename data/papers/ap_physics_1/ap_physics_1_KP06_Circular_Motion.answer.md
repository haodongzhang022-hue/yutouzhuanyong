## 📖 参考答案与解析

**1. A car of mass 1000 kg goes around a circular track of radius 50 m at 20 m/s. What is the centripetal force required?**

- 答案：`B`
- 解析：Fc = mv²/r = 1000×20²/50 = 1000×400/50 = 8000 N.

**2. An object of mass 0.5 kg moves in a circle of radius 2 m with a speed of 4 m/s. What is the centripetal acceleration?**

- 答案：`B`
- 解析：ac = v²/r = 16/2 = 8 m/s².

**3. A 1000 kg car rounds a curve of radius 50 m at 20 m/s on a level road. What is the minimum coefficient of friction needed? (g = 10 m/s²)**

- 答案：`C`
- 解析：f = mv²/r = μmg → μ = v²/(rg) = 400/(50×10) = 0.8.
