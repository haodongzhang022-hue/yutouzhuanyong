## 📖 参考答案与解析

**1. A straight wire carrying 5 A current is placed in a 0.2 T magnetic field perpendicular to the wire. If the wire is 2 m long, what is the magnetic force on it?**

- 答案：`C`
- 解析：F = BIL = 0.2 × 5 × 2 = 2 N.

**2. A coil has 200 turns and a magnetic flux changes from 0.05 Wb to 0.01 Wb in 0.1 seconds. What is the induced EMF?**

- 答案：`C`
- 解析：ε = -NΔΦ/Δt = -200×(0.01-0.05)/0.1 = -200×(-0.04)/0.1 = 80 V.
