## 📖 参考答案与解析

**1. Two forces of magnitude 6 N and 8 N act at right angles to each other on an object. What is the magnitude of the resultant force?**

- 答案：`A`
- 解析：Pythagorean theorem: √(6²+8²) = √(36+64) = 10 N.

**2. A 4 kg object is accelerated at 3 m/s². What net force is acting on it?**

- 答案：`D`
- 解析：F = ma = 4×3 = 12 N.
