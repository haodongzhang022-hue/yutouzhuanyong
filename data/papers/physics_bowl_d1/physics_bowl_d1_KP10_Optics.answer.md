## 📖 参考答案与解析

**1. A concave mirror has a focal length of 10 cm. An object is placed 20 cm in front of the mirror. Where is the image formed?**

- 答案：`C`
- 解析：Using mirror equation 1/f = 1/do + 1/di: 1/10 = 1/20 + 1/di → 1/di = 1/20, di = 20 cm in front (real).

**2. Light passes from air (n=1.0) into glass (n=1.5) at an angle of incidence of 30°. What is the angle of refraction? (sin⁻¹(0.333) ≈ 19.5°)**

- 答案：`A`
- 解析：Snell's law: n₁sinθ₁ = n₂sinθ₂ → sinθ₂ = (1.0×sin30°)/1.5 = 0.5/1.5 = 0.333 → θ₂ ≈ 19.5°.
