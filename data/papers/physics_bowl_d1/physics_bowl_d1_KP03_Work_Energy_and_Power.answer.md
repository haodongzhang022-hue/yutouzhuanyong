## 📖 参考答案与解析

**1. A 3 kg object is lifted vertically to a height of 4 m. How much work is done against gravity? (g = 10 m/s²)**

- 答案：`C`
- 解析：W = mgh = 3 × 10 × 4 = 120 J.

**2. A 2 kg object moving at 5 m/s collides with a stationary object of equal mass and sticks to it. What is the kinetic energy lost in the collision?**

- 答案：`B`
- 解析：Initial KE = ½×2×25 = 25 J. After: v' = mv/(2m) = 2.5 m/s, KE' = ½×4×6.25 = 12.5 J. Lost = 25-12.5 = 12.5 J.
