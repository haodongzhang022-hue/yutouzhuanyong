## 📖 参考答案与解析

**1. A car accelerates uniformly from rest to 20 m/s in 5 seconds. What distance does it travel?**

- 答案：`C`
- 解析：Using x = ½at² and a = v/t = 20/5 = 4 m/s², x = ½×4×25 = 50 m.

**2. A projectile is launched at 30° above horizontal with speed 40 m/s. What is its maximum height? (g = 10 m/s²)**

- 答案：`B`
- 解析：v_y = v·sin30° = 40×0.5 = 20 m/s. h = v_y²/(2g) = 400/20 = 20 m.

**3. A ball is thrown vertically upward with speed 30 m/s. After 4 seconds, what is its displacement? (g = 10 m/s²)**

- 答案：`B`
- 解析：y = v₀t - ½gt² = 30×4 - ½×10×16 = 120 - 80 = 40 m upward.
