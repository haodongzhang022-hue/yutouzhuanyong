## 📖 参考答案与解析

**1. The half-life of a radioactive isotope is 10 days. How much of a 100 g sample remains after 30 days?**

- 答案：`B`
- 解析：After 3 half-lives (30/10=3): 100 × (½)³ = 100/8 = 12.5 g.

**2. An electron in a hydrogen atom transitions from n=3 to n=2. What happens to the emitted photon energy? (Eₙ = -13.6/n² eV)**

- 答案：`A`
- 解析：ΔE = E₃-E₂ = -13.6/9 - (-13.6/4) = -1.51 - (-3.4) = 1.89 eV.
