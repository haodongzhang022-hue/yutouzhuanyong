## 📖 参考答案与解析

**1. Sound waves in air are what type of wave?**

- 答案：`B`
- 解析：Sound travels as longitudinal (compression) waves.

**2. A wave has a frequency of 2 Hz and wavelength of 1.5 m. What is the wave speed?**

- 答案：`C`
- 解析：v = fλ = 2×1.5 = 3 m/s.

**3. A string fixed at both ends has a fundamental frequency of 100 Hz. What is the frequency of the second harmonic?**

- 答案：`C`
- 解析：Second harmonic = 2× fundamental = 2×100 = 200 Hz.
