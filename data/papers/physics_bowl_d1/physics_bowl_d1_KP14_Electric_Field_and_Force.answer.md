## 一、单选题（共 1 题）

> 从 A/B/C/D 中选择唯一正确答案。

**1. A charge of 4 μC is placed in an electric field of 500 N/C. What is the force on the charge?**

- A. 0.002 N
- B. 0.02 N
- C. 0.2 N
- D. 2 N

---


## 📖 参考答案与解析

**1. A charge of 4 μC is placed in an electric field of 500 N/C. What is the force on the charge?**

- 答案：`A`
- 解析：F = qE = 4×10⁻⁶ × 500 = 2×10⁻³ = 0.002 N.
