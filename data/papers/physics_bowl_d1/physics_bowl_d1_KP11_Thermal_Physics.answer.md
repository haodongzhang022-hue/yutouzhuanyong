## 📖 参考答案与解析

**1. An ideal gas is compressed adiabatically from volume 4 L to 2 L. The initial pressure is 1 atm. What is the final pressure? (γ = 1.4 for diatomic gas)**

- 答案：`C`
- 解析：For adiabatic process: P₁V₁^γ = P₂V₂^γ, P₂ = P₁(V₁/V₂)^γ = 1×(4/2)^1.4 = 2^1.4 ≈ 2.64 atm.

**2. How much heat is needed to raise the temperature of 2 kg of water from 20°C to 70°C? (specific heat of water c = 4200 J/(kg·°C))**

- 答案：`B`
- 解析：Q = mcΔT = 2×4200×50 = 420,000 J = 420 kJ.
